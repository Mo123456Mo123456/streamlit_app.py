## 🧠 Understanding AI Limitations

### What AI Can Do:

✅ Find patterns in historical data
✅ Perform calculations quickly
✅ Generate statistical summaries
✅ Create visualizations
✅ Make predictions based on past trends

### What AI Cannot Do:

❌ Predict the future with certainty
❌ Account for unknown unknowns
❌ Understand context like humans
❌ Exercise judgment or wisdom
❌ Guarantee any outcomes
❌ Replace human expertise

### The "Automatic Profit" Myth:

Many people ask for "AI systems that automatically generate profits." Here's the truth:

1. **If it existed, everyone would use it** - and then it wouldn't work anymore
2. **Markets are competitive** - easy profits get arbitraged away instantly
3. **Risk always exists** - higher returns require higher risk
4. **Complexity is necessary** - real trading systems are incredibly sophisticated
5. **Scams are common** - most "automatic profit" claims are fraudulent
6. **No free lunch** - in finance, there are no guaranteed easy profits

This tool was created to educate about what AI actually can do, in contrast to false promises.
